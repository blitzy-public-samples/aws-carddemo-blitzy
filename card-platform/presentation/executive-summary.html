Independent reactions

Figure 2 — One outcome event starts the work

Figure 2 shows one event starting three independent reactions. No arrow joins the three consumers. Detail in docs/event-flow.md.

graph LR
  accTitle: Figure 2, one outcome event starts the work
  accDescr: A REST client calls authorization-service, which publishes transaction.authorized and transaction.declined. Three consumers read transaction.authorized independently: ledger-posting-service, fraud-detection-service and notification-service. The ledger also reads transaction.declined and records one reject row for each of the three reasons a decline carries, 0101, 0102 and 0103. Reason 0100 is refused before any decision is taken, so no event carries it. Each decline is keyed on the account it applies to. The ledger publishes transaction.posted, which notification-service and account-service both read, and fraud-detection-service publishes fraud.assessed, which notification-service reads. A record the ledger, fraud or notification listener cannot complete travels to its own source topic plus the .DLT suffix, and account-service routes one to the shared carddemo.dead-letter fallback. No consumer calls another consumer.
  C["REST client"] --> A["authorization-service"]
  A ==>|approved| TA{{"transaction.authorized account key"}}
  A ==>|declined| TD{{"transaction.declined"}}
  TA ==> L["ledger-posting-service"]
  TA ==> F["fraud-detection-service"]
  TA ==> N["notification-service"]
  TD ==> L
  L ==> TP{{"transaction.posted"}}
  F ==> FA{{"fraud.assessed"}}
  TP ==> N
  TP ==> AC["account-service"]
  FA ==> N
  L -.-> DLT{{"source topic plus .DLT"}}
  F -.-> DLT
  N -.-> DLT
  AC -.-> SH{{"carddemo.dead-letter fallback"}}
